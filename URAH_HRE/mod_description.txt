The campaign map replaced by the Holy Roman Empire at about 2.6x the vanilla detail: 302 provinces
from the Low Countries to Hungary and from Denmark to northern Italy, with real terrain, rivers,
roads and mountains, and three historical starting periods (1110, 1224, 1360) with the Empire's
princes, prince-bishops, free cities and Italian communes.

Requires the URAH_HRE and URAH_MapKit BepInEx plugins (see README / INSTALL.txt). Without it this mod does nothing.
Do not activate together with URAH Iceland. Experimental.
